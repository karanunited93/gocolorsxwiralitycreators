Go Colors · Brand ambassador launch

Life moves in your comfort zone.

Go Colors onboarded its first-ever brand ambassador and repositioned from a leggings-first label to India's complete women's bottomwear destination. The film carried the story on CTV and streaming. We made it personal through the women living it every day.

The brief

Comfort is not where life slows down. It is where life moves.

The brand film carried the idea at scale. The influencer layer had a different job: make "comfort that moves with you" feel lived-in and relatable, through real women's workdays, wardrobes and weekends.

That meant content that integrates the campaign thought into how creators already talk to their audience, not a straight product ad.

Curated shortlist

Micro and nano creators matched to three audience profiles, with rates, for fast approval.

Native content

Workwear styling, day-in-the-life and comfort-led routines built around the campaign thought.

Whitelisting rights

90-day paid usage on every piece, so the best reels could be amplified through performance marketing.
